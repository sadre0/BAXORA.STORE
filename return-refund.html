Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

At BAXORA, customer satisfaction is important to us. This policy explains the conditions for returns, replacements, cancellations and refunds for orders placed with BAXORA.

1. Return Period

Eligible non-customized products may be returned within 7 days from the date of delivery, including when the customer changes their mind, subject to the conditions in this policy.

2. Return Conditions

  • The product must be unused and in its original condition.
  • Original tags, packaging and included accessories should be returned wherever applicable.
  • The product must not have damage caused by misuse, alteration or handling after delivery.
  • Order details or proof of purchase may be requested.

3. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact BAXORA within 7 days of delivery. After reviewing the issue, BAXORA will arrange an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement or refund.

4. Customized Products

Customized products are not eligible for return due to a change of mind or personal preference. If a customized product is delivered damaged, defective or different from the confirmed order, contact us within 7 days of delivery. BAXORA will review the issue and arrange an appropriate resolution.

5. Return Shipping Charges

BAXORA will bear the return shipping cost for approved returns and eligible replacement or refund requests. Customers will not be charged a return courier fee for an approved return under this policy. Please contact us and wait for return instructions before sending a product.

6. How to Request a Return

Contact us through WhatsApp or email with your order details, product name and reason for the request. We may ask for photographs or other information to review the request.

7. Refunds

Once the returned product is received and inspected, BAXORA will inform you about the outcome of the request. Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method wherever possible. If that is not available, an alternative method will be mutually agreed. The time taken for the refund to appear may depend on the bank or payment provider.

8. Order Cancellation

You may request cancellation by contacting BAXORA before your order is dispatched. If advance payment has been made, an approved pre-dispatch cancellation will be refunded. Cancellation after dispatch is not guaranteed.

9. Delivery

BAXORA accepts orders for delivery across India. Delivery charges are included in the displayed product price unless expressly stated otherwise at the time of order confirmation. Estimated dispatch is within 1–3 working days after order confirmation, and estimated delivery is 5–10 working days. Actual delivery may vary due to location, courier operations, weather or other circumstances beyond our control.

10. Your Legal Rights

This policy does not limit any rights or remedies available to consumers under applicable Indian law. Where applicable law provides a greater protection, the law will prevail.
